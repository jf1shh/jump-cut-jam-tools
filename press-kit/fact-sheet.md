# Jump Cut: fact sheet

Source: the game page text. Team: jf1shh.

## What it is
The jam theme is "Director's Cut", and in Jump Cut you make one. You are the stunt driver on a film shoot: drive each stunt, or hold and let go to cut past the ones you cannot make, and a plain cut costs film. At the wrap you can play the Director's Cut, the same scene again with the stunts you cut put back in.

## How it plays
- A stunt car runs down a desert road closed for a film shoot. Every stunt on the call sheet (SHOT 1/7, SHOT 2/7 and on) is a take.
- Thread the car past each stunt as close as you dare. A late, close pass gets a PERFECT stamp.
- Crash, and the slate reads RETAKE! and you go back to the start of that shot.
- Cannot make it? Hold the cut button. Time slows, the picture drains to black and white, and a small inset shows where you will land. Let go and the film cuts you past the stunt. A plain cut costs one piece of film.
- Film is your budget for cuts: a CLOSE pass earns one piece back and a PERFECT earns more.

## Controls
- Steer with A / D (or the left stick). Hold W / S (or RT / LT) to go faster or slower.
- Hold Space (gamepad: A) to cut past a stunt, then let go.
- Gamepad plays the whole game: stick to steer, A to cut, X for the stunt, START to pause, Y for settings.
