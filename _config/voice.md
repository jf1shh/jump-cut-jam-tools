## Quick reference

- Plain words, short sentences, one idea per line; say what was done and what is next.
- Evidence standard: a claim about the game needs a build number, screenshot or log path (`stages/05_devlog/output/evidence.md`).
- Dev log (Glass Box) is honest about AI use: what agents did, what the owner did, what failed.
- English only, teen-rated content, no third-party IP (Rules 7(i), 8, 15(d)).
