## Q2. Judging criteria, scoring, weighting and tiebreak

### Takeaway
A judging panel reportedly scores each qualified entry from 1 to 5 on seven criteria: **Ambition and innovation, Theme, Fun factor, Gameplay, Visuals, Level design and game progression, Audio**. AI use is reportedly **not** a scored criterion. No weighting or tiebreak was found. These criteria come only from WindowsForum's relay of the itch page.

### Cited Findings
- "A panel scores each qualified entry from 1 to 5 on: Ambition and innovation; Theme; Fun factor; Gameplay; Visuals; Level design and game progression; Audio." AI use is not scored — [WindowsForum relaying itch.io](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/) (secondary).
- Epic's own forum posts do not cover judging criteria. A promotional post by community member mimmo_the_root claims there are seven criteria, which matches the count but is not confirmed by Epic — [Epic forum thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/vibe-your-way-to-50k-the-2026-epic-ai-megajam-is-coming/2843973).
- Epic's FAQ says online and in-person entries are judged the same way — [Epic forum thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/vibe-your-way-to-50k-the-2026-epic-ai-megajam-is-coming/2843983) (primary).
- Development Continuation Grants go to entries that a human judge thinks are worth continuing. No rubric is given — [EGW.news](https://egw.news/gaming/news/37966/epic-games-announces-ai-megajam-then-drops-the-ai--84xEnla5b) (secondary).
- Historical context from Epic: Epic says it uses a "robust rubric that dives into the specifics of each criteria," with judges from inside and outside Epic. "Scores have historically not been released across challenges and jams hosted by Epic Games." Some sponsored categories factor in the sponsor's choices — [Epic forum, 2025 MegaJam update](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/2025-epic-megajam-mega-stack-o-jam-update/2689696?page=2) (primary, earlier jam).
- 2023 MegaJam precedent (Epic official): judges scored 1–5 on "Unique Use of Contest Theme", "Fun Factor", "Visuals", "Audio" and "Gameplay". **"Sourced assets will not be reviewed and scored,"** but how they are implemented can still earn points, for example spatialization or real-time modulation of sourced audio — [Fortnite.com / Epic](https://www.fortnite.com/news/the-epic-megajam-is-open-to-uefn-submissions) (primary, earlier jam).

### Inferences
- Compared with 2023, the 2026 list adds **Ambition and innovation** and **Level design and game progression**. That points toward a short game with a clear start, escalation and end, rather than a single-room toy.
- If the 2023 rule about sourced assets still applies (unconfirmed for 2026), visuals and audio built from Fab or packaged assets earn little unless they are visibly customized. Lighting, post-processing, VFX and audio mixing are where points can still be earned.
- Scores will probably not be published, so there will be no feedback beyond placement.

### Gaps
- Verbatim criterion descriptions, any weighting, the tiebreak rule, judge names and panel size were all not found. The itch page and the Rules PDF could not be opened.
- Whether the 2023 sourced-asset scoring rule carries into 2026 is unknown.

## Q3. Prize categories, amounts, cap, and the $65k vs $50k conflict

### Takeaway
Cash prizes total **$65,000**. Each of the three main categories (Best Overall UE, Best Overall UEFN, Best Student Project) pays **$7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500**. The **$50k / $25k / $10k** figures are **Development Continuation Grants**, not cash prizes. "$50,000 prize pool" headlines misread the grant ceiling. An entry can win one main category plus one modifier. For a solo UE entrant who is not a student, the most cash available is **$7,500 + $2,500 = $10,000**, plus possibly a grant.

### Cited Findings
- Main categories pay $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500 for 1st/2nd/3rd: Best Overall Unreal Engine; Best Overall UEFN; Best Student Project (UE or UEFN, for students enrolled at a higher-education institution) — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/); [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27578/epic-games-announces-2026-epic-ai-megajam-with-up-to-50000-in-grants); [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114250/epic-games-2026-ai-megajam-offers-dollars65000-in-prizes-but-the-ai-requirements-are-confusing/index.html) (all secondary, consistent with each other).
- Modifier categories, as relayed from itch.io and Epic — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/):
  - **Best Team**: $5,000
  - **Solo Driver** (best game by one person): $2,500
  - **Render Royalty** (visuals in an **Unreal Engine** game): $2,500
  - **Pocket Powerhouse** (mobile): $2,500 to each of one UE winner and one UEFN winner
  - **Glass Box** (best dev log): $2,500. [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27578/epic-games-announces-2026-epic-ai-megajam-with-up-to-50000-in-grants) describes it as "best dev log showing AI tool use".
  - **Walkie-Talkie** (narrative **UEFN** games with LLM-powered NPCs): $2,500
- Same modifier names confirmed independently — [EGW.news](https://egw.news/gaming/news/37966/epic-games-announces-ai-megajam-then-drops-the-ai--84xEnla5b).
- Cap: each submission can win one main and one modifier category. TechRadar's example is a Best Overall winner who also wins Best Team and collects $12,500 — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/); also [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27578/epic-games-announces-2026-epic-ai-megajam-with-up-to-50000-in-grants).
- "$65,000" cash total, attributed to itch.io — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/Generative_AI/item-52480); [TweakTown](https://tweaktown.com/news/114250/epic-games-2026-ai-megajam-offers-dollars65000-in-prizes-but-the-ai-requirements-are-confusing/index.html).
- **Conflict:** [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151119/epic-games-ai-megajam-contest) and [Mezha](https://mezha.ua/en/news/epic-games-ai-game-tournament-316478/) headline a "$50,000 prize pool". WindowsForum says Epic's slogan "Vibe your way to $50K" refers to the grant ceiling, and that TechRadar's "$50,000 prize pool" appears to be a misreading — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/).
- **Conflict on the user's brief:** the brief recalled reports of UE 1st/2nd/3rd prizes of $50k/$25k/$10k. No source supports that as cash. WindowsForum relays itch as listing those amounts as grant ceilings — see Q4.

### Inferences
- Arithmetic check: 3 main categories × ($7,500 + $5,000 + $2,500) = $45,000. Modifiers add $5,000 + $2,500 + $2,500 + (2 × $2,500) + $2,500 + $2,500 = $20,000. **Total $65,000**, which matches the reported cash total exactly and confirms the $7.5k/$5k/$2.5k reading.
- For a **solo UE** entrant, the realistic categories are:
  - Main: Best Overall UE, or Best Student only if Jared is enrolled in higher education.
  - Modifier, one of: Solo Driver, Render Royalty, Pocket Powerhouse (UE, which needs a mobile build), or Glass Box (dev log).
  - Not available: Best Team (implicitly needs a team; unconfirmed) and Walkie-Talkie (UEFN only).
- Only one modifier can pay out. Even so, entering several modifier angles (solo, visuals, a dev log) increases the chance of winning at least one. Whether entrants must name their modifiers on the form is unconfirmed for 2026; the 2023 form required it (see Q5).

### Gaps
- Whether entrants must opt into modifiers, and whether one entry can be considered for several modifiers, is not confirmed for 2026.
- Whether Solo Driver can be paired with a main-category win is unconfirmed beyond the general "one main + one modifier" rule.
- Prize payment method was not found. A forum user mentions Hyperwallet, which is unconfirmed.

## Q4. Development Continuation Grants

### Takeaway
Grants are separate from cash prizes. They are reportedly up to **$50,000 (1st), $25,000 (2nd) and $10,000 (3rd) in each of Unreal Engine and UEFN**, and go to "standout" entries for work after the jam. They are discretionary, and no selection criteria or eligibility rules were published in any source opened.

### Cited Findings
- "Development Continuation Grants (itch.io): Separate from cash, for continued work on the game. Up to $50,000 (1st), $25,000 (2nd) and $10,000 (3rd) in each of Unreal Engine and UEFN. Epic says these go to 'standout' entries, so they are not guaranteed." — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/) (secondary relay of itch).
- Epic's own wording: "development continuation grants for standout entries", with the FAQ saying grants are "up to $50,000" for standout UE and UEFN entries — [Epic forum thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/vibe-your-way-to-50k-the-2026-epic-ai-megajam-is-coming/2843983) (primary).
- "Up to $50,000 per team for further development" — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/Generative_AI/item-52480) (secondary).
- Grants go to projects a human judge thinks are worth continuing. The number of grants is not stated — [EGW.news](https://egw.news/gaming/news/37966/epic-games-announces-ai-megajam-then-drops-the-ai--84xEnla5b) (secondary).
- Work can continue after the jam, but the jam version is the build that gets judged — [Epic forum thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/vibe-your-way-to-50k-the-2026-epic-ai-megajam-is-coming/2843983) (primary).

### Inferences
- The grant tiers mirror 1st/2nd/3rd, so the UE Best Overall placings probably map to grant eligibility. That is unconfirmed: Epic says only "standout" and "up to".
- An entry that clearly reads as the seed of a larger game (a strong core loop and a visible path to expand) probably helps with grant consideration, given the "worth continuing" framing.

### Gaps
- Grant selection criteria, whether grants are tied to placement, payout terms (milestones, contract), and eligibility are all missing. The Official Rules could not be read.

## Q5. Submission requirements and every rule that could disqualify an entry

### Takeaway
For a UE entry, as relayed from itch and Epic:
- Upload a **packaged development build** for Windows, macOS, Android or iOS. Unpackaged projects are not accepted.
- Use a **ZIP named `TeamName_GameName` under 1 GB**. Larger builds go up through butler or the Blendo Itch Uploader.
- Upload to your own itch account, then submit to the jam.
- Provide team name, each member's name and Epic-linked email, the game name, and a **30–60 second gameplay video link**. A dev log is optional except for Glass Box.

Other rules:
- Entrants must be 18 or older with an Epic Games account.
- Teams can have up to 5 people.
- A team uses one engine only.
- No building before Nov 12. **All pre-jam prompts, scripts and assets must be declared.**
- Third-party or Fab assets are allowed if licensed **and listed**.
- Gameplay must go beyond Epic starter templates.
- Infringing or inappropriate content is barred.
- Late entries are disqualified.

Country restrictions exist in the Official Rules (users cite Italy and Türkiye), but the Rules text was not read.

## Q8. Epic's prep recommendations (agentic tools and MCP)

### Takeaway
Epic tells entrants to set up tools, agents and MCP **before** kickoff, and to "connect your agents to the editor with Unreal MCP". It does not link a specific guide in the posts that were opened. The official doc is "Unreal MCP in Unreal Editor" on dev.epicgames.com. It covers an **Experimental** plugin documented for **UE 5.8** that serves MCP at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/mcp` and can generate client configs for Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini and Codex.

### Cited Findings
- "Use any AI tools you like, and connect your agents to the editor with Unreal MCP." Tools, agents and MCP can be set up before Nov 12 — [Epic forum thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/vibe-your-way-to-50k-the-2026-epic-ai-megajam-is-coming/2843973) (primary). The thread links no official MCP documentation.
- Epic recommends setting up agentic coding tools and MCP in UE and UEFN before kickoff — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27578/epic-games-announces-2026-epic-ai-megajam-with-up-to-50000-in-grants). Epic is promoting MCP tools with setup guides and livestreams — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114250/epic-games-2026-ai-megajam-offers-dollars65000-in-prizes-but-the-ai-requirements-are-confusing/index.html).
- Tips relayed from the itch page:
  - Get MCP working before kickoff.
  - Keep scope small and prioritize polish.
  - Package or publish a test build on day two.
  - Keep notes if entering Glass Box.

  Source: [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/epic-ai-megajam-2026-ai-is-optional-65k-cash-prizes-and-unreal-engine-submission-rules.449768/) (secondary).
- Official doc: [Unreal MCP in Unreal Editor](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/unreal-mcp-in-unreal-editor).
  - The plugin is internally named `ModelContextProtocol`, is Experimental, and the doc targets UE 5.8.
  - Setup: enable the **Unreal MCP** and **All Toolsets** plugins and restart. Turn on Auto Start Server (default `http://127.0.0.1:8000/mcp`). Run `ModelContextProtocol.GenerateClientConfig` to write `.mcp.json` to the project root. Launch the agent from the project root, or use the optional Terminal plugin to run it inside the editor.
  - Supported clients: ClaudeCode, Cursor, VSCode, Gemini and Codex.
  - Limits: HTTP/SSE only, no auth, loopback only. Tools run serially on the game thread, so avoid overlapping calls. Live Coding does not pick up new `UFUNCTION` tools.
- Unofficial community kit (not Epic): https://github.com/mimmo-the-root/UEFN-dream-bot-team — [Epic forum thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/vibe-your-way-to-50k-the-2026-epic-ai-megajam-is-coming/2843983).

## Q9. Previous Epic MegaJams: criteria, themes, winners, and what made winners win

### Takeaway
Past MegaJams were 7-day jams with 1–5 panel scoring on theme, fun, visuals, audio and gameplay. Winners took ambiguous themes literally but distinctively, and placed in both a main category and a visual or technical modifier.

Epic's winner posts contain almost no judge rationale. The evidence for "why they won" is thin, and the patterns below are inferences from winner lists.

### Cited Findings
**Criteria history**
- 2023 (Epic official): scored 1–5 by a panel on Unique Use of Contest Theme, Fun Factor, Visuals, Audio and Gameplay. Sourced assets were not scored.
  - Epic's tips were to focus on the gameplay loop and lean into the theme.
  - There were 11 modifiers, for example Army of One (solo), Audio Experience, Make Us Go WOW (Photoreal/Stylized), and Speed of Light (NVIDIA, 60+ FPS).
  - Teams could win one primary category and one modifier.

  Source: [Fortnite.com / Epic](https://www.fortnite.com/news/the-epic-megajam-is-open-to-uefn-submissions).
- 2025: Epic used a "robust rubric", and scores were never released. Categories included RTX On (NvRTX), Digital Brush Mastery (hand-drawn visuals) and Army of One — [Epic forum](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/2025-epic-megajam-mega-stack-o-jam-update/2689696?page=2).

**Themes and scale**
- 2020: "It's been a long time, but we're not done yet" — [JungleFall devlog](https://tree-industries.itch.io/junglefall/devlog/203808/post-megajam-thoughts).
- 2021: "Running Out of Space", with 504 submissions and over 3,000 participants. Epic noted entrants' different takes on "space" (land management, shrinking rooms, cats' preferences) — [Epic blog 2021 winners](https://www.unrealengine.com/blog/presenting-the-winners-of-the-2021-epic-megajam).
- 2022: "As Above, So Below" (duality, literal and figurative). Seven days, teams of up to 5 — [Epic blog 2022 winners](https://www.unrealengine.com/blog/presenting-the-winners-of-the-2022-epic-megajam). The jam ran 2022-08-25 to 2022-09-02 with 509 entries per the itch snippet — [itch post](https://itch.io/post/6572792/view-in-topic).
- 2025: "Here We Go Again" (10-year anniversary), with optional classic themes Running Out of Space, Space Cats and "However Vast the Darkness…" — [X post by @ImmatureGamerX](https://x.com/ImmatureGamerX/status/1978944998805770551) (secondary). It ran 2025-10-16 to 2025-10-25 with 491 entries — [itch.io 2025 jam (search snippet)](https://itch.io/jam/2025-epic-megajam).

**Winners**
- 2021: 1st BotiBoi (Purple Team), which **also won Nvisionary Environments**. 2nd Seekers (Microwasp), which **also won The KitBasher and the Tiny Award**. 3rd The Rat Way Home (Flying Bear). Army of One went to Container City (Perfoon) — [Epic blog](https://www.unrealengine.com/blog/presenting-the-winners-of-the-2021-epic-megajam).
- 2021 BotiBoi origin: a five-person team took the theme as software collecting files before the system crashes. It was a simple arcade platformer with a strong visual metaphor ("miniature cities" inside computers). The team went on to form a studio and ship *Boti: Byteland Overclocked* — [Pad and Pixel](https://padandpixel.com/?p=5150).
- 2022:
  - 1st Fregward: Spirit Warden (Catfrog), which **also won Tiny But Mighty**.
  - 2nd Beyond Outbreak.
  - 3rd Backstage Shenanigans (Backstage Crew), which **also won Make Us Go WOW (Stylized)**.
  - Army of One: The ResT (Tashkas).
  - Audio Experience: Below Decks.

  Source: [Epic blog](https://www.unrealengine.com/blog/presenting-the-winners-of-the-2022-epic-megajam).
- 2023 (announced Nov 30 on Inside Unreal):
  - UE 1st Rolling Back, 2nd Neo-Siege, 3rd The Lost Slipper.
  - Student 1st Solaris, which **also won Audio Experience**.
  - Army of One: Lamp Lighter.

  Source: [Unreal Source](https://unrealsource.com/d/2023-epic-megajam-winners/).
- 2025: RTX On 1st Glitch In The Labyrinth (nocttokki). Digital Brush Mastery went to Kronos, a repeat winner. Winners were announced on Inside Unreal around March 2026 — [Epic forum](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/2025-epic-megajam-mega-stack-o-jam-update/2689696?page=2).
- 2015 (first MegaJam): Epic said "Judging has never been so difficult." It gave no per-winner rationale — [Epic blog 2015](https://www.unrealengine.com/blog/epic-megajam-winners-announced).

**Postmortem lessons (non-winners or placement unknown)**
- 2021 Bit Barrage:
  - Scope crept as new abilities, enemies and a boss were added.
  - Building the boss in Blueprints and then rewriting it in C++ wasted "4 hours of work".
  - Less difficulty tuning was done than planned.
  - Some features "didn't really do much for the game".

  Source: [Bit Barrage postmortem](https://patrickdean.itch.io/bit-barrage/devlog/292175/epic-megajam-2021-postmortem).
- 2020 JungleFall: reached "about 80% of what I had in mind". Leaving the menu to the end left the game with no clean exit and restart bugs. Migrating assets to a newer engine version cost time — [JungleFall devlog](https://tree-industries.itch.io/junglefall/devlog/203808/post-megajam-thoughts).

### Inferences
- **Main winners often also win a visual or technical modifier.** Examples: BotiBoi (Environments), Seekers (KitBasher), Fregward (Tiny But Mighty), Backstage Shenanigans (Stylized WOW), Solaris (Audio). Under 2026's cap, the stronger play for a solo UE entrant is to aim at Best Overall UE plus Render Royalty or Solo Driver.
- **The theme is read literally and with a strong single metaphor** (BotiBoi's "software city"). Epic praised the range of distinct interpretations of an ambiguous theme. "Theme" is one of the seven 2026 criteria.
- **Small and finished beats big and broken.** Both postmortems cite scope creep and late menus or UX as regrets, and Epic's 2026 tips repeat "small scope, polish, test build on day two".
- Category names like "Tiny But Mighty" and "Tiny Award" show Epic rewarding compact, polished games.
- The 2026 jam is 4 days, down from 7 in earlier years, so scope pressure is higher than in past MegaJams.

### Gaps
- No judge commentary explaining why specific winners won was found in any Epic winner post (2015, 2021, 2022). The 2023 post and the 2025 winners announcement are VOD-only.
- 2024 MegaJam theme and winners were not found.
- Theme and criteria for 2016–2019 were not researched due to the tool-call budget.
- The 2022 theme-announcement forum post returned only metadata, so 2022 criteria are unconfirmed.
- No itch winner-postmortem from a first-place team was opened. The postmortems found are from entries whose placement is unknown.
