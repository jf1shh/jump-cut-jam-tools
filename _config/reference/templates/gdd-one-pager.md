# GDD one-pager — [Game Name]

Fill this in once, at the start of jam week, after the theme drops. Keep it to one page: if an answer
needs more than 2-3 sentences, it belongs in a feature-slice brief instead, not here.

- **Pick:** leader and runner-up with their rubric totals (`scoring-rubric.md`), one line.
- **Risk fork:** the one thing that must work at the Thu 01:00 day-1 gate, the cheap mitigation, and the
  safe fallback.
- **Theme tie-in:** how the Contest Theme shows up, ideally on the title screen and in minute 1 (Rule 7(g)).
- **Core verb:** the one thing the player does, over and over, that's fun on its own in greybox.
- **Loop:** the one sentence that describes a full playthrough, start to end screen.
- **Win/lose states:** what ends a run, in either direction.
- **Fun Factor bet:** why this will be fun by day 2's "played by someone else" gate, specifically —
  not "it'll be fun once it's polished."
- **Visual direction:** one reference image or phrase; the one master material per hero look.
- **Scope cut list:** the first 3 things to cut if day 3 runs long. Write this now, not at 10 PM Saturday.
- **Out of scope:** anything you're deliberately not building (multiplayer, saves, settings beyond
  the minimum Rule 7(f) needs).
