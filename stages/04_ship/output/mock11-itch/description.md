# Helium Balloon (mock jam build)

A small red helium balloon is tied to a kid by a string. Walk the kid down a windy street fair, catch all 5 ribbons, and keep the balloon away from the spikes.

**Controls:** W A S D (or arrow keys) walk the kid. R restarts.

**Goal:** catch 5 ribbons. Your time shows on the win screen.

**Known issues:**
- Early mock build: engine shapes for the stalls, a few real props.
- No sound yet.
- The win time shows raw decimals.
- The title line also appears on the pop screen when you have 0 ribbons.
