## One thing that surprised us
A clean pass of automated Blueprint-graph QA (`read_graph_dsl` confirming event wiring is correct)
gave false confidence that the build was fine. It wasn't: the player pawn had no movement component to
consume its own movement input, so it was a guaranteed silent no-op regardless of what the graph
looked like. Graph wiring checks are necessary but not sufficient — nothing beats an actual human
playing the actual packaged build.
