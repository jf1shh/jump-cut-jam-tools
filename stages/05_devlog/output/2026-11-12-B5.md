# Devlog note: build B5, the wrap fix (2026-11-12, jam night 1)

Jam progress only. B5 is on the private pretest page (itch build 2364016, version 25).

## What broke in B4
The test on the Legion handheld caught one bug. When the film wrapped, the results screen came up but the
scene clock kept running behind it. The timer climbed past 60 and the picture behind the card went to a black void.

## The fix
Once the scene is wrapped, the clock stops. B5 holds the results card with the timer frozen
(`T 37.0` in the first screenshot, still 37.0 fifty seconds later). Space, A or a click plays again, and the
restart works after a wrap. Every splice between takes now gets a short burst of film grain.

## What the Legion test showed
Win, lose and restart all passed on the handheld with no errors in the log: a wrap with 4 points and 2 stars,
a crash on take 1, and a restart after the wrap. Pause (Esc/P) and the free retake also passed.

## Still rough (next build)
- The timer is still visible on the results card (frozen).
- Overlays sit on the car, and orange "CUT!" over the orange street front is hard to read.
- Gamepad Start/A is not yet tested on the real pad.
