## What broke

- **The wordless box.** A prompt plate painted with no words. It is pixel-identical with every switch off, so it is a
  bug in the base. Saved with the fault named and fixed in a later round.
- **The canal wall material never compiled.** A custom shader named a variable `line`, which is a reserved word. The
  walls are matte concrete, lighter than expected.
- **Grey horizon cliffs.** A material was made from a material that Unreal refuses as a parent, so about two thirds of
  the cliffs and spires drew the engine's default grey. A fix is cut and reviewed.
- **The ford.** Three versions of a wet road failed their looks. The ruling: stop chasing it after two tries. A dry
  causeway is what shipped.
- **The oncoming truck.** Revision 1 slid sideways. Revision 2 still crabbed up to 64 degrees and lurched from 0 to
  14 m/s in half a second. Both were rejected. Revision 3 is in review.
- **The truck-steer pop.** The truck body still jumps about 250 pixels in one frame. Three fixes were tried; one was
  reversed for a failing test and the next one replaced it.
- **Saves 148 and 152 went in over a looker's "STOP".** Both times the looker's STOP because the fault was in the
  base code, not the patch. That rule is written down and used on purpose.
- **A one-frame test line** (S1-R LATE) shows up as an old flake: 3 of 3 reruns passed; 0 of 12 logs on recent trees.
