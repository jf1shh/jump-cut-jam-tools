# Connection / growth — five candidate concepts

2026-11-07. Planning proposals requested by Jared, not selected entries or evidence of the contest theme. Working titles only. Create no shipping gameplay, levels or art before the permitted window. Read AGENTS.md, RULES.md and PLAN.md; include pre-jam planning in the declaration as applicable.

| Candidate | Repeated player decision | Main appeal | Biggest four-day risk | Minimum complete cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Root Circuit](../output/root-circuit-concept.md) | Which root can I reclaim to power the next path? | Compact spatial/resource puzzles and a living circuit | Clear previews and safe structural retraction | Two chambers, bridge + lock, Heartseed ending |
| [Living Grapple](../output/living-grapple-concept.md) | When do I release, and where does my next route grow? | Expressive traversal and a playable garden of past crossings | Swing/camera tuning | Two terraces, four guided crossings, permanent routes and return |
| [Bloom Brigade](../output/bloom-brigade-concept.md) | Where do I assemble, cross and recall my companions? | Memorable characters and visible formation changes | Formation transitions plus ball handling | Two courts, bridge only, six creatures and final bloom |
| [The Last Signal](../output/the-last-signal-concept.md) | Which relay pad completes the visible signal chain? | Spatial discovery with a restrained human/audio story | Invisible occlusion rules or dull walking | Two yards, one repeater, permanent doors and captioned broadcast |
| [Overgrowth Delivery](../output/overgrowth-delivery-concept.md) | Which seed do I deliver next, given the routes it changes? | A coherent small town transformed by the player | Travel time and navigation after closures | Two destinations, one opening/closure and rooftop ending |

Each full concept defines its pitch, theme reading, controls, exact interaction/state rules, complete run, recovery, visual/audio budget, technical responsibilities, four-day gates, acceptance checks, cuts and presentation plan. The implementation sections are proposals to validate against pinned engine references, not tested API claims.

## Selection procedure

1. At reveal, write how the actual theme changes decisions in each plausible candidate. Discard cosmetic fits.
2. Use [the repo rubric](../../../_config/reference/templates/scoring-rubric.md). Scores are estimates; do not award invented proof of fun. Fun Factor and Gameplay must meet its thresholds.
3. Keep one clear runner-up. Select the smallest concept that can deliver its payoff within the existing PLAN.md build gates.
4. Once the contest window opens, prove the chosen core verb in greybox and a Windows build. Do not start five prototypes.
5. Apply the concept's explicit fallback when its named risk fails the day-1/day-2 gate. Preserve an ending, readable feedback, recovery and submission time.

Initial design judgment: Root Circuit remains the balanced baseline. The Last Signal is an alternative if movement engineering needs to stay modest; Living Grapple is the largest movement bet; Bloom Brigade offers the strongest creature identity; Overgrowth Delivery emphasizes world transformation. These are comparative design judgments, not judging scores or tested outcomes.

## Pre-jam boundary

This shortlist and the five specifications are design documents only. They do not select a final name, change contest rules, authorize early game construction, generate assets or prove theme fit. The actual theme and final candidate decision remain pending.
