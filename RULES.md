## Timeline

- Contest period: 2026-11-12 12:00 AM ET to 2026-11-15 8:59 PM ET (Rule 4).
  In Pacific time: Wed Nov 11, 9:00 PM to Sun Nov 15, 5:59 PM.
- Theme: announced at the start, on Epic's Unreal Engine livestream and X account (Rule 1).
  The itch.io page lists the kickoff livestream as Nov 11, 11 AM PT.
- Winners notified on or about 2027-01-29; 72 hours to respond with Epic Account ID and Prize Release (Rule 14).

## Eligibility (Rules 1, 5)

- 18+, active Epic account registered to you, not a Cabined Account.
- Resident outside the Restricted Jurisdictions: Iran, Iraq, North Korea, Somalia, Sudan, Syria, and
  the regions of Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk (void by law/policy); Italy (registration/filing
  requirements); and anywhere else prizes can't legally be distributed. As of the Oct 26, 2026 rules
  update, Brazil, Russia and Turkey are no longer Restricted Jurisdictions — **but entrants who
  reside in Brazil are separately excluded from cash prizes specifically** (non-cash prizes and
  participation still allowed).
- Teams of 1 to 5 entrants; one Team Owner receives prizes for the team.
- Not relevant to Washington residency, but for completeness: content creators/influencers engaged
  by Epic for a single project or campaign remain eligible to enter and win, as long as they meet all
  other eligibility rules.

## Our track: Unreal Engine (Rule 2: one submission per team; UE or UEFN, not both)

- Packaged for Windows and playable on Windows 10 or 11, macOS, Android or iOS (Rule 1(b)).
- Should not require any additional software to run (Rule 7(f); "should", not "must" in the Nov 5 PDF; treat it as a must anyway).
- Not an obvious extension of an Epic tutorial; custom gameplay beyond the starter templates (Rule 7(d), (e)).
- Must demonstrate use of the Contest Theme (Rule 7(g)).
- itch.io page: zip named `TeamName_GameName`, under 1 GB, or upload with butler. Build type: the submit page
  says "We recommend a development build for all platforms." and "Shipping builds are also accepted for Windows,
  macOS, and Android." (read live 2026-11-14), so either a development or a Shipping Windows build is allowed.

## Submission contents

- Full name and email of every entrant, one named Team Owner (Rule 1(c)).
- Sourced (non-original) assets noted (Rule 1(d), 15(c)).
- Video, 30–60 seconds of recorded gameplay, 1920×1080 or higher (Rule 1(e), 7(c)).
- Everything in English: in-game text, audio, video, entry info (Rule 7(i)).
- Dev log, submitted with the entry during the contest period, for Glass Box (Rule 10).
- Submitted by a human, not an AI agent or automated means (Rule 17).

## AI and pre-jam work

- AI use is optional and allowed for code, assets and in-game systems (Rule 7(a)).
- Before the contest period, only AI tools, agent configurations, MCP settings, prompt libraries and
  scripts may be prepared. Sponsor may ask for them or their version-control history. All gameplay,
  levels and art must be created during the contest period (Rule 7(h)).
- AI-generated content is not a sourced asset (Rule 15(c)).

## Content (Rules 8, 15)

- Teen audience (13+), no infringing, hateful, obscene or disparaging content.
- No trademarks except the Unreal Engine logo, per Epic's branding guidelines (Rule 15(d)).
- Sourced assets must be licensed and listed, and **will not be reviewed or scored** (Rule 15(c)).

## Judging (Rule 9)

Seven criteria, up to 5 points each: Ambition and Innovation, Use of Contest Theme, Fun Factor,
Gameplay, Visuals, Level Design, Audio. The extent of AI use disclosed is context only and does not by
itself raise or lower a score. Ties are re-scored with double weight on Fun Factor.

## Prizes (Rules 10, 11, 13)

- **Limit: one main-category prize and one modifier prize per submission** (Section 13, "Limit one (1)
  main category prize and one (1) Modifier Prize per Submission"). A winner can win only one modifier
  category unless no other eligible winner remains for the extra one (Section 10).
- Glass Box (Section 10): the dev log must be submitted together with the Submission during the Contest Period.
- Best Overall, Unreal Engine: $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500.
- Best Overall, UEFN: $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500 (not our track).
- Best Student Project: $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500 (all entrants must be enrolled in higher education).
- Modifiers: Solo Driver $2,500; Best Team $5,000 (2+ entrants); Render Royalty $2,500; Pocket
  Powerhouse $2,500 each (one UE, one UEFN); Glass Box $2,500 (dev log); Walkie-Talkie $2,500 (UEFN only).
- Our modifier target: **Glass Box**. Solo Driver is the fallback we're also eligible for.
- Development Continuation Grants (UE: up to $50,000 / $25,000 / $10,000) are by invitation after the
  contest, not prizes, and not guaranteed.
- Total cash prizes: $65,000.
